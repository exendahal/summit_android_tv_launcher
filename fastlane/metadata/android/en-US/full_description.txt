Summit is a calm, fast home screen for Android TV. It puts live TV one press away, keeps your favourite apps in reach, and shows the time, date and weather at a glance. It is built to stay light, even on TV boxes with 1 GB of memory.

<b>Home</b>
• Watch TV first: one press of OK opens your TV input (HDMI, AV, antenna) or your live-TV app.
• Continue: the apps you used last, with how long ago.
• Favourites: pin your apps, then hold OK to move or unpin them.
• A large clock with the date, an optional regional calendar date and a daily health tip.
• Weather at a glance, with a full forecast one click away.
• Voice search and the TV's own settings right from the header.

<b>Apps</b>
Every installed TV app in one alphabetical grid. Hold OK or press Menu to pin, move, open app info or uninstall.

<b>Make it yours</b>
Settings show a live preview of Home beside them, so every change is visible as you make it.
• Theme: automatic by time of day, dark or light.
• Wallpapers: built-in illustrations, community mountain wallpapers, or a picture from your TV.
• Colour taken from your wallpaper, or one of 9 colours, with adjustable contrast.
• Text size, tile size and shape, and app-name display.
• Weather for your current location or any city, in °C or °F.

<b>Ambient screensaver</b>
A quiet clock with the date, weather and health tip over a faded wallpaper. It moves every minute to protect your screen from burn-in and dims at night.

<b>Languages</b>
English, Nepali, Hindi and Chinese (Simplified), with local date formats, regional digits and an optional regional calendar.

<b>Quick setup</b>
A short guided setup picks where you watch TV, your favourite apps, wallpaper, look and language. Every choice can be changed later in Settings.

<b>Privacy</b>
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your app usage and pictures never leave the TV.

Summit is open source under the MIT License.

Requires Android TV or Google TV running Android 8.0 or newer. Some Google TV devices do not allow another home screen; Summit then still runs from the app list.
